Bolyra Playground

Two views: Verify receipts checks a pasted receipt or receipt log for signature validity, expected signer, chain integrity against an external checkpoint, and instance binding. Simulate spend policy makes simulated allow/deny decisions and signs real chained receipts for them with a temporary key.

Verification runs the published @bolyra/receipts@{{RECEIPTS_VERSION}} in your browser. Nothing you paste is sent anywhere. Verify exports with npx @bolyra/cli@{{CLI_VERSION}} receipt verify-chain.
